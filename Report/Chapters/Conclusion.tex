\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This thesis set out to mechanise the syntax, semantics, and metatheory of \pill{} in Lean~4. The aim was not to extend the source calculus, but to provide a precise mechanised account of an existing theoretical framework by making its treatment of binding, freshness, linear resources, and operational behaviour explicit within a proof assistant.

The formalisation covers the static structure of the full \pill{} calculus, including session types, processes, environments, hyperenvironments, and typing judgements. The binding architecture retains explicit free identifiers while representing bound occurrences by indices: channel binders are managed through locally nameless opening and co-finite premises, whereas polymorphic type binders are handled through type-depth indexing, shifting, and substitution. This representation avoids explicit reasoning up to $\alpha$-equivalence for bound occurrences while preserving readable free channel names in processes, typing hyperenvironments, and transition labels.

The operational semantics is mechanised for the multiplicative fragment \pimll{} through three related transition systems: a primary transition relation over typing derivations, a projected process transition relation, and a projected environment transition relation describing the corresponding evolution of hyperenvironments. This organisation retains the derivation-level semantic architecture of the source theory while adapting its correspondence results to Lean's inductive-relational setting.

For the mechanised fragment, the development establishes the completed projection directions of the session-fidelity argument: typed derivation steps induce both process and environment transitions. The reverse lifting theorem, \texttt{typability\_subject\_reduction}$_\texttt{m}$, is complete for the multiplicative transition cases except \texttt{res}. Consequently, the remaining obstacle to completing the targeted subject-reduction argument for \pimll{} is concentrated in the restriction case, where a transition of an opened body must be lifted while recovering the two endpoint-bearing hyperenvironment components required to reconstruct the enclosing \texttt{cut} derivation.

A central insight of the mechanisation is that choices which simplify the representation do not remove proof complexity, but relocate it. The locally nameless treatment of channel binders removes the need to reason explicitly about $\alpha$-equivalent bound names, but introduces opening, local closure, and co-finite freshness infrastructure. Likewise, the paper presentation can treat fresh representatives and the rearrangement of unordered linear resources implicitly, whereas the Lean development must connect co-finite typing premises to the concrete names exposed by process transitions and must express hyperenvironment rearrangement through explicit permutation and extraction lemmas. The incomplete \texttt{res} case exposes this shift particularly clearly: the difficulty is not selecting fresh restricted endpoints or opening the body, but recovering the post-transition resource decomposition needed to reintroduce the restriction.

The development also identifies limitations and extensions beyond the current mechanisation. Environments and hyperenvironments are represented by lists modulo permutation, which supports structural induction but places a substantial burden on permutation-sensitive proofs. Process-level structural congruence is not yet formalised, so syntactic rearrangements available in the paper presentation are not automatically available to the mechanised transition systems. Beyond completion of session fidelity for \pimll{}, extending the dynamic semantics to full \pill{} and mechanising readiness, productivity, and the broader behavioural metatheory remain future work.

Overall, the thesis provides a substantial mechanised account of the static theory of full \pill{} and the operational metatheory of its multiplicative fragment. It confirms that the source theory's derivation-level erasure and session-fidelity architecture can be represented in Lean, establishes the completed parts of that architecture, and isolates the structural infrastructure required to close its principal remaining proof obligation.
